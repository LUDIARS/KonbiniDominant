# Phase 1–4 campaign baseline (2026-09-09)

The user requested Phase 2–4 and an Aion entrance WARNING, after delegating
remaining implementation judgment. BASE-CAMPAIGN-01 below makes the previously
unresolved rules concrete for this playable build. Numbers are required content
v4 fields, not silent code defaults. Historical v2/v3 profiles retain their rules.

## Progression
Chain selection remains exactly one of the three playable chains for the entire
run. Phase 1 domination OR its five-minute timer advances to Phase 2; the campaign
does not end on the old timed score comparison. No stores and no rebuilding
capital loses before progression. Phase 2 lasts three minutes or ends when one
lot has 256 contiguous occupied slots. Phase 3 ends after two foreign dimensions
are annihilated or conquered. A five-second WARNING precedes the thirty-second Aion window.
The warning does not consume the manifestation window. Pause stops all clocks.
All earlier mechanics remain active; retry resets the complete campaign.

## Vertical invasion — BASE-CAMPAIGN-VERTICAL-01
Every buildable lot supports 256 slots, each 3.2 metres high. A new upper store
requires an occupied slot directly below it and may belong to another chain.
Existing upper stores remain floating when support is destroyed; rebuilding holes
requires support again. Slots never renumber. Price is the base cost plus 1 percent
of base cost per slot, rounded upward. Ground reconstruction remains available.
Population loss applies to ground replacement, not every added floor.
Faith ranges from zero to 100, starts at 50, and updates once per economy period.
Natural growth, bounded friendly/rival neighbors, triangle support and height
advantage determine its change. Image strategy spends cash, has a cooldown and
increases the player's stores' faith. Maximum faith doubles a store's circle
influence from Phase 2 onward. Other stores can reach full faith naturally.
Vertical presentation uses bounded floor bands and a floor jump, rather than
rendering all 256 individual floors at full detail from street level.

## Multiverse — BASE-CAMPAIGN-DIMENSION-01
Dimensions share the immutable lot graph and geometry generated by Figmentum.
Each gets unique facility/population IDs and a deterministic ordinal-derived seed
for population and invader placement, plus a distinct presentation tint.
This instancing choice avoids synchronous polygonization in the frame loop.
Cross-dimension anchors are exact Figmentum facility keys plus vertical slot.
Float proximity is never a match. Both foreign worlds are populated on entry to
Phase 3; all active worlds simulate at the same tick rate.
An inversion command targets a non-boss rival in a foreign world at maximum
faith, spends its configured cost and obeys its cooldown. The owner does not
change. The anti-store annihilates with a matching player store in the origin
world. Both stores are destroyed and the foreign world collapses in the same
transaction. The origin receives a timed income/faith buff; a second buff refreshes
duration instead of multiplying repeatedly. Collapsed worlds reject commands
and publish no world objects. IDs are retained as inactive tombstones.
A foreign world with player stores and no surviving rival stores is also conquered
and collapsed with the same origin energy reward. This prevents combat from
removing every inversion target and soft-locking progression.

## Aion — BASE-CAMPAIGN-AION-01
Aion is an unselectable fourth faction with seven named presentation forms
(the original Aion/retainer roster), using the same placement/occupancy rules.
Content supplies its invasion budget, cadence and time-attack parameters.
It builds across active worlds every 0.4 seconds. Its dominant triangle starts
a four-second MaxValue warning. Breaking that triangle cancels the warning;
expiry collapses the dimension. 9223372036854775807 is display payload, not HP math.
Time cut/paste takes the recorded boss layout from two seconds earlier and pastes
up to three stores at a deterministic lot offset every five seconds. Occupants
at the paste targets are removed in the same tick before boss placement. This is
an actual world-state attack, not a visual rewind.
Escape spends construction cash and atomically creates a seeded world plus one
player store, with a three-second cooldown and six active-world budget.
Ordinals continue increasing; destroyed worlds do not consume the active budget.
Boss destruction/collapse resolves before the survival check, including on the
last tick. Zero player stores in active worlds loses immediately; at least one
after exactly thirty seconds wins. The entrance warning is outside those thirty seconds.

## Controls and validation
The UI exposes floor selection/top-floor jump, repeat construction, image strategy,
dimension cycling, inversion and escape with reason text/costs/cooldowns.
WARNING is a large high-contrast centered banner with countdown, not merely a HUD
line. MaxValue warnings identify their dimension and remaining time.
Build and archive integrity verification are authorized. Unit/integration/game
startup tests remain unexecuted unless the user explicitly requests them.
